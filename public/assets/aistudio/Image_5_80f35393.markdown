## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, technical blueprint aesthetic engineered specifically for an avant-garde recording artist and visionary music video director. It synthesizes architectural schematic drafting, optical engineering notations, and brutalist editorial discipline. The visual mood evokes an active production laboratory or high-fidelity sound stage: clinical, exact, yet deeply visceral and artistic.

### Design Movement & Core Principles
- **Brutalist Schematic Drafting:** High-structure grid lines, corner reticles, technical datum markers, and drafting metadata tags (`SHEET 01`, `REV A`, `FPS 24`, `TIME 03:45`, `ISO 800`) establish structural rigor.
- **Cinematic Production Rigor:** Surfaces emulate blueprint stock, high-grade cinematic call sheets, and archival production logs. 
- **Disciplined Precision:** Elimination of decorative soft drop shadows in favor of razor-sharp hairline borders (0.5px–1px), structural coordinate labels, and calibrated information architecture.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an archival, warm drafting paper base contrasted against carbon black inks and urgent production markers.

- **Primary (`#111111`):** Heavy carbon ink used for razor-sharp typography, technical dividers, solid production pill badges, and primary interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#FF4D22`):** Signal orange-red utilized for active track indices, camera recording indicators (`REC`), hot links, and visual focal nodes.
- **Tertiary (`#D9B98C`):** Warm drafting vellum used for delicate blueprint grid matrices, hairline perimeter frames, and secondary architectural callouts.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F2`):** The foundational base canvas evoking uncoated fiber stock and physical studio boards.

### Application Logic
- **Backgrounds:** Primary viewports default to `canvas-cream` (`#FAF7F2`), transitioning to `surface-cream-alt` (`#F3EFE6`) for modular schematic cards. Dark terminal viewports or cinematic screening rooms shift to `surface-dark` (`#16181D`).
- **Data & Text:** Content text remains locked to `technical-black` (`#111111`) for maximum contrast and typographic legibility. Sub-labels and machine metadata adopt `code-gray` (`#555555`).
- **Grids & Dividers:** Subtle coordinate grids and schematic section borders leverage `border-subtle` and `border-grid`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a strict tripartite pairing:

1. **Editorial Display (`EB Garamond`):** Delivers literary, high-art authority for film and track titles, album monographs, and high-impact manifesto headlines.
2. **Structural Clarity (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Provides pristine, unadorned grotesque neutrality for narrative body copy, tracklists, bios, and navigational links.
3. **Machine Telemetry (`Space Mono`):** Injects raw technical precision across timestamps, aspect ratios, focal lengths, production sheet numbers, and status indicators. Always uppercase for labels and tags.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a calibrated 12-column architectural grid with visible hairline guidelines and reference benchmarks.

### Structural Framework
- **Canvas Framing:** Top-level viewports are anchored inside a full-bleed outer container framed with a subtle border and corner alignment crosses (`+`).
- **Grid Alignment:** Columns adhere to strict proportional divisions (12-column desktop, 6-column tablet, 2-column mobile). Major sections feature explicit datum markers (e.g., `DATUM 00 120.000 // POS X`) positioned along dividing horizontal rules.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8px base grid. Section transitions demand disciplined breathing room (`space-2xl`), while technical metadata chips sit tightly (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) against viewport headers.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids blurred drop shadows and ambient lighting models. Visual depth is strictly orthographic and structural:

- **Surface Layering:** Depth is created through surface contrast (e.g., nesting `#F3EFE6` or `#16181D` panels over the base `#FAF7F2` canvas).
- **Hairline Framing:** Crisp 1px solid borders in `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.12)` or `rgba(217, 185, 140, 0.35)` delineate stacked panels.
- **Precision Technical Markers:** Panels integrate corner bracket notches (`⌜`, `⌝`, `⌞`, `⌟`) or technical crosshairs to establish structural positioning without blur or artificial z-axis casting.
- **Active Selection Depth:** Active or selected items receive high-contrast fills (solid `#111111` or `#FF4D22`) rather than elevated drop shadows.

## Shapes

The primary shape philosophy is strictly **Sharp (0px)** to preserve the blueprint and engineering terminal aesthetic. 

- **Containers & Cards:** 0px radius with visible hairline borders.
- **Badges & Terminal Chips:** Micro pill shapes (full radius) are reserved solely for high-contrast technical metadata pills (`SHEET 01`, `REV A`) to mirror clinical camera tape labels and instrument chassis toggles. All structural modules, media frames, and buttons maintain crisp right angles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#111111` background, `#FAF7F2` uppercase `Space Mono` typography, 0px border radius, 12px 20px padding. Hover state shifts instantly to `#FF4D22` with zero transition lag.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#111111` hairline, technical black text. Hover state inverts to full `#111111` fill with `#FAF7F2` text.
- **Terminal Action:** Monospace pill container with dot indicator (`● PLAY [03:45]`).

### Technical Metadata Badges & Chips
- Segmented pills composed of adjacent cells enclosed in a single 1px `#111111` shell.
- Left cell: Inverted solid black block with white monospace sheet code (`SHEET 02`).
- Right cell: Cream background with code status in carbon black (`REV B // 4K LOG`).

### Media & Video Containers
- 16:9 and 2.39:1 anamorphic ratio containers encased in hairline borders.
- Top banner bar features live metadata telemetry: timecode counter (`00:02:14:18`), lens metadata (`35MM T1.5`), and camera status indicators (`● REC`).
- Corner boundary ticks overlaying the media preview frame.

### Cards & Modular Panels
- Enclosed with 1px `border-subtle` or `border-grid`.
- Headers contain a dedicated technical utility bar separated by a horizontal rule.
- Modular panels support inverted "Dark Room" modes (`surface-dark` background with white text and signal orange highlights) for video playback and audio waveform displays.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat fields with bottom hairline rules or full crisp bounding boxes.
- Floating monospace labels placed above the input line (`PARAM // VALUE`).
- High-contrast square checkboxes (0px radius) with an uncompromising solid fill when checked.
